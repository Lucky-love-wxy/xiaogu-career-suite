中心简介

首都医学科学创新中心[Chinese Institutes for Medical Research (CIMR), Beijing]（简称创新中心）是北京市新成立的具有独立法人资格的新型研发机构。创新中心以推动医学科学发展、改善人类健康为目标，开展生物医学研究，致力于提升医学科学创新与成果转化能力，提高疾病诊断和治疗水平。我们将汇聚世界高水平科学家，与首都医学教育和科学研究优质资源紧密合作，打造多学科交叉融合的科研平台，综合自由探索、医学目标导向、有组织科研等途径，实践新型科研模式和体制机制，培养适应医学科学创新发展的优秀人才，逐步推进医教研产的深度融合。

实验室介绍

Yun实验室团队以蝾螈为模型系统，致力于揭示器官再生的细胞与分子机制，解析演化差异对再生潜能的影响，并探索再生与衰老的深层关联。我们采用两种具有基因操作可行性的蝾螈模型 墨西哥钝口螈（美西螈，Ambystoma mexicanum）和西班牙肋突螈（Pleurodeles waltl），这两种生物以极强的再生能力和几乎可以忽略的衰老特征而著称。通过整合前沿遗传学技术、细胞分子生物学方法、先进成像系统、多组学分析与跨物种比较研究，我们致力于揭示再生与衰老的基本规律，推动基础研究与治疗应用。

2. 库存维护与档案记录；

3. 常规设备维护及实验室日常运营；

4. 新成员实验技术与设备操作培训；

5. 协助动物实验伦理审批及许可证办理；

6. 开展基础分子/细胞生物学实验、图像采集与数据分析；

7. 参与合作研究并独立承担课题。

二、任职要求

1. 分子生物学、生物化学或相关专业学位；

2. 具备实验室管理经验；

3. 熟练掌握以下分子生物学技术：

? 基因克隆与编辑

? 细胞培养

? 显微成像技术（共聚焦/立体荧光/光片显微镜等）

? 组织处理（样本制备、切片及染色技术，包括免疫组化IHC/IF、常规/HCR原位杂交）

4. 有动物实验与转基因操作经验者优先；

5. 素质要求：

? 卓越的组织协调能力

? 对细节的高度关注

? 流利的英语口语与书面表达能力

一、岗位职责

二、任职要求

3. 素质要求：

生物信息分析师

一、岗位职责

1. 设计并开展单细胞及群体水平的生物信息学分析，包括：

? 基于10XMultiome工作流程和Illumina测序平台生成的mRNA测序数据分析

3. 解析空间转录组数据；

4. 参与科学假说提出、实验设计、分析流程文档编写、研究汇报及论文撰写；

5. 为团队成员提供技术培训与指导；

6. 协调独立工作与团队协作。

二、任职要求

1. 生物信息学或相关领域学位；

2. 扎实的生物信息学理论基础及实践经验，尤其擅长：

? 单细胞数据分析

? 空间转录组学分析

3. 必备流利的英语听说读写能力；

4. 有再生生物学、衰老研究或进化生物学经验者优先。

ABOUT CIMR

Chinese Institutes for Medical Research (CIMR), Beijing（首都医学科学创新中心）is a research organization dedicated to advancing basic and translational medical research. Our goal is to tackle fundamental problems in human health with cutting-edge technology and through interdisciplinary collaborations with world-class scientists and physicians in Beijing. CIMR will provide education and training opportunities for future medical scientists. We are committed to a science-centered framework to promote innovation in research and education through a variety of novel funding mechanisms.

ABOUT YUN LAB

The Yun group leverages salamander systems in order to unveil the cellular and molecular mechanisms underlying organ regeneration, determine how phylogenetic changes affect regenerative potential, and probe the links between regeneration and ageing. We employ two genetically-tractable salamander models, the axolotl (Ambystoma mexicanum) and the Iberian ribbed newt (Pleurodeles waltl), known for their extreme regenerative abilities and negligible senescence traits. Combining state-of-the-art genetics, cell and molecular biology approaches, advanced imaging, multiomics and cross-species comparative studies, we aim to uncover fundamental principles of regeneration and ageing towards basic and therapeutic applications. We offer an international, creative, science-driven and inspiring environment. The successful candidates will benefit from our large network of collaborations (Germany, UK, USA) and have immediate opportunities to contribute to international projects. Our lab is located at the newly built Institute for Regenerative Biology and Medicine, Chinese Institutes for Medical Research.

Lab Manager

We are seeking a skilled scientist to manage our newly established laboratory.

Main Responsibilities

a. Order equipment, reagents and lab consumables.

b. Maintain lab stocks and records.

c. Maintain general lab equipment and organisation.

d. Train new staff on common lab techniques and equipment.

e. Assist with animal protocols and licenses.

f. Perform basic molecular/cell biology, image acquisition and data analysis.

g. Conduct research collaboratively as well as independently.

Qualifications

Candidates will have a degree in Molecular Biology, Biochemistry or related subject, experience with general lab management and a strong background on molecular biology approaches including cloning, gene editing, cell culture, microscopy (e.g. confocal, stereo-fluorescence and/or light sheet), tissue processing including preparation, sectioning and staining techniques (e.g. immunostainings IHC/IF and standard/HCR in situ hybridization). Experience in animal research and transgenesis is desirable. Soft skills include outstanding organisational skills, keen attention to details and fluency in spoken and written English.

Molecular Biology Technician

We are seeking a highly skilled Molecular Biologist to join our efforts to unravelling the links between regeneration and ageing. He/she will conduct research collaboratively as well as independently.

Qualifications

The successful candidate will have a strong background on molecular biology approaches including state-of-the-art gene editing, cloning methods, CRISPR/Cas-9 technologies, mTol2 or equivalent systems, animal transgenesis, cell culture, tissue processing including preparation, sectioning and staining techniques (immunostainings IHC/IF and standard/HCR in situ hybridization). Prior experience with regeneration models and/or surgical procedures is a bonus. Soft skills include keen attention to details and fluency in spoken and written English.

Bioinformatician

We are seeking a highly motivated bioinformatician to join our efforts to unravelling the links between regeneration and ageing.

Main Responsibilities

a. Design and conduct bioinformatic analyses of data at single cell and bulk level, including transcriptomic, epigenetic (e.g. DNAm arrays, genomic data) and proteomic datasets, including mRNA sequencing data generated via the 10XMultiome workflow and Illumina sequencing.

b. Conduct cross-species single cell analyses.

c. Analyse and interpret spatial transcriptomics.

d. Contribute to hypothesis generation, experimental design, analytical pipeline documentation, research presentations and manuscript preparation.

e. Provide training/guidance to other team members.

f. Balance independent work and team collaboration.

Qualifications

Candidates should have a degree in bioinformatics or a related field, strong theoretical knowledge and experience in bioinformatics, particularly single cell data and spatial transcriptomics analyses. Fluency in spoken and written English is required. Prior experience in the fields of Regeneration, Aging or Evolutionary biology is a bonus.

Postdoctoral Researcher

We are seeking outstanding, highly motivated postdoctoral researchers to join our efforts to unravelling the links between regeneration and ageing.

Qualifications

Candidates should have a Ph.D. degree in Biology, Biochemistry, Computational Biology or related, an outstanding academic track record, and fluency in spoken and written English. A strong background and research experience in Regenerative, Ageing, Developmental or Evolutionary Biology is desirable. Ideally, candidates would be trained in animal experimentation (not exclusive), tissue culture, bioinformatics and cell/molecular biology techniques.

Welfare Treatment

a. Competitive salary based on the job applicant's work experience and abilities (salary negotiable).

b. Social insurance, paid annual leave, physical examination and supplementary medical insurance, etc.

c. Opportunities for career development and available professional guidance.

How to apply

Please send a resume, a brief summary of research interests & key accomplishments, the names and contacts of 3 referees and other materials demonstrating research capabilities to max.yun@, and indicate the applicant's name and specific job position in the email subject.
