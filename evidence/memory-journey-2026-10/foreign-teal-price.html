Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a return or refund policy?

If you are not satisfied with your purchase, you may request request a refund by contacting our team at membersupport@tealhq.com with the following information:
‍
• Reason for cancellation
• Email associated with the account where the payment was made

Refund requests will only be considered if they are submitted within your plan’s eligibility timeframe. Outside of these periods, your purchase would be ineligible for a refund. Review our full cancellation and refund policy to see what applies to your plan.

Are there any hidden fees or charges?

Teal offers clear, transparent pricing with no hidden fees or unexpected charges. While most of Teal's tools are available for free, we offer Teal+ for job seekers who want to take their job search to the next level and unlock the full functionality of our Resume Builder.

What are the benefits in upgrading to Teal+?

Starting with the base product is an excellent way to get used to the process and create a free professional resume. However, with Teal+, you can get even deeper with the AI-powered resume builder functionality, unlimited advanced resume analysis, resume keyword matching, and more.  Job seekers who leverage Teal are getting out applications faster and landing more interviews.

How can I cancel my subscription once I've landed a job?

To cancel your subscription, head to your account settings and click on Teal+ Subscription, from there you'll be able to view your current plan and cancel your paid subscription. Once you cancel your recurring subscription, you won't be charged for future subscriptions. You can submit your cancellation at any time, however the cancellation will take affect at the end of your current billing period. Teal cannot be held responsible for recurring subscriptions if a member fails to cancel their account.

Please confirm your subscription is cancelled by reviewing your account settings - if your subscription is cancelled, you will see a red banner underneath your current plan with a date that shows when your subscription will be cancelled, upon which your account will revert back to the free plan.
