LUCK IS ABOUT BEING OPEN + CURIOUS.
Research (Richard Wiseman) suggests that those who call themselves
“lucky” tend to look beyond the task given, to question to premise, and
to actively seek out new ways of doing things.

CAUTION: THE “OR NOT” BINARY
 • “Should I do this or not?” is a clue that you may be trapped in a
   binary decision.
   • Ex. Accept the job offer I got, or not?

 • Change the ”not” into options you might do, based on your criteria.
    • Ex. Accept the offer as it stands or…
       • Negotiate the terms of the offer; or,
       • Say no and conduct more info interviews; or …
       • Defer start date by 3 months to pursue lifelong dream of
           climbing Mt. Everest; or …

CYCLE OF NON-REGRET                                     GATHER
                                                        & CREATE

Dr. Afia Ofori-Mensa
1.   Make the best decision you can with the
     information you have at the time.
2.   Resist the temptation to judge past-you with the
     information that present-you received after that
     decision was made.
3. Recognize that you are who you are, you can only
   be you, because everything in your life has
   happened in precisely the way and exactly the
   order in which it has happened.
4. Love yourself.
5. Trust that future-you can handle the results of
   whatever decisions present-you makes.

FRAMING PITFALLS
Don’t prototype deciding – prototype the thing you want more info about
in order to decide.
How might I prototype deciding to do a sabbatical?
How might I prototype being on sabbatical?


Don’t mix 3-4 questions into 1.
How might I prototype taking a job at a small liberal arts college while raising 2 kids as a single parent
and owning a house?
• How might I prototype working at a small liberal arts college? OR
• How might I prototype single parenting away from my family? OR
• How might I prototype home ownership?


Don’t ask should or either / or questions! Pick one thing to prototype!
Should I take a job in tech? or in academia?
How might I prototype working in tech?
